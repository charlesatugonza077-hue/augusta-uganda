Privacy

Simple, respectful use of enquiry details.

Details shared through the booking form are used to respond to your enquiry and check service availability.

What we collect

Name, selected service, location, timing and the details you choose to share. Please do not include passwords, payment details or highly sensitive information.

How the website enquiry works

The website prepares a message from the details you enter. When you select Continue to WhatsApp, your device opens WhatsApp with that message for you to review. The message is not sent by the website automatically. You decide whether to send it by pressing Send in WhatsApp.

Who receives your information

If you send the message, it is shared with Augusta through WhatsApp. WhatsApp is operated by a third party and its own privacy terms apply to information handled by that service. Do not include passwords, payment credentials or sensitive personal information.

How Augusta uses enquiries

Augusta uses information you choose to send to respond, check availability, prepare a quote and arrange a service. Access should be limited to people who need the information to handle your request. Augusta should retain enquiry details only as long as reasonably needed for those purposes and applicable legal obligations.

Your questions and requests

For a question about information you have shared directly with Augusta, contact us through the WhatsApp link on this website and identify the enquiry. Requests about information held by WhatsApp must be directed to WhatsApp through its own privacy controls.

Questions?

Contact Augusta directly on WhatsApp.
