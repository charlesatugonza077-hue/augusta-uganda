What the plan covers: one scheduled weekly visit for four weeks. The UGX 200,000, UGX 300,000 and UGX 450,000 plans apply to studio/one-bedroom, two-bedroom and three-bedroom apartments respectively. Each plan includes agreed house cleaning, mopping, bathroom cleaning and scrubbing, utensil washing and light wardrobe folding or organisation. Mobile laundry is a separately paid add-on. Need another visit that week? We can add an extra visit, subject to availability and a separate quote. Standalone residential houses: we first visit the property, assess the scope, then provide a tailored quote.
