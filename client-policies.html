Before you pay or confirm

Augusta will confirm the service, date, location, price or pricing basis, exclusions, payment terms and cancellation route in writing.

Booking, payment and changes

For Augusta Home, the proposed UGX 30,000 commitment fee is credited toward the first service payment. It is not automatically non-refundable. If you need to cancel or reschedule, contact us early. We will assess notice, preparation or travel, the reason and applicable law, then explain the outcome in writing.

Home service scope

Weekly apartment visits include agreed cleaning, mopping, bathroom cleaning and scrubbing, utensil washing and limited light wardrobe support. Laundry is a separately paid add-on.

Privacy and creative media

Enquiry details are used to respond, quote, arrange and deliver service. Portfolio use of photographs or video requires the appropriate agreement or consent, with extra safeguards for children and sensitive events.

Concerns and property

Tell us promptly if something is wrong. We will review service, property or payment concerns fairly, using the booking record and available evidence.

Important: These proposed policies are pending Ugandan legal review and Director approval. They are not yet final contract terms.
