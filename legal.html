Legal and policy information

Clear, fair and still under review

Augusta is building a formal policy system for its creative and household services. This page explains what applies while the proposed customer documents are being reviewed by Ugandan counsel.

Current status

The client policy centre, proposed terms of service, booking and payment guidance, privacy notice and service-specific procedures are proposed documents. They become effective only after legal review, written Director approval, staff implementation and consistent publication.

Customer protections

Augusta aims to confirm service scope, timing, price and key conditions in writing before accepting a booking. We will provide agreed services with reasonable care and skill and will not use blanket wording to remove rights that cannot lawfully be excluded.

Home bookings

The proposed UGX 30,000 Augusta Home commitment fee is credited toward the first service payment. It is not presented as automatically non-refundable. Cancellations, rescheduling, access problems and Augusta-initiated changes must be assessed fairly against the confirmed booking and applicable law.

Data, photos and video

Augusta uses customer information to respond, quote, arrange and deliver services. Public portfolio use of images or video requires the appropriate agreement or consent, with heightened safeguards for children and sensitive events.

Questions or concerns

Ask before you book or contact Augusta promptly if you have a service, payment, property or privacy concern. We will review it using the booking record and available evidence.
