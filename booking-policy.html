Before confirmation

Augusta will state the service scope, price or pricing basis, payment timing and any approved travel or add-on charges before confirming a booking.

Augusta Home commitment fee

The proposed UGX 30,000 commitment fee is paid before a Home booking is confirmed and credited toward the first service payment. It is not automatically non-refundable.

Changes, cancellation and access

Contact Augusta as early as possible. We assess notice, confirmed preparation or travel, the reason for change and applicable law, then explain the outcome in writing. If Augusta cannot deliver a confirmed service, we will offer a reasonable reschedule, replacement or refund outcome as appropriate.
