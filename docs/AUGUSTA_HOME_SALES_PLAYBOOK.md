# Augusta Home sales playbook\n\n## Goal\n\nHelp a household choose a simple fixed monthly plan quickly. Sell the result, not hours: a cleaner, calmer and more organised home on a reliable routine.\n\n## Tailored Home Care rules\n\nUse **Tailored Home Care** only when a standard plan cannot honestly cover the home. It is still a single monthly price, never an open-ended hourly charge.\n\n### Move a lead to Tailored Home Care when one or more apply\n\n- More than three bedrooms, multiple floors or more than one separate household.\n- The home has frequent visitors, children, several pets or a level of use that makes normal-plan cleaning unrealistic.\n- The client wants four or more visits and more rooms than the Weekly Home Care scope can cover.\n- A first reset is heavy: accumulated clutter, long-unattended cleaning, move-in/out condition, or several wardrobes/storage areas.\n- The address is outside Augusta’s core service route.\n- The client asks for a mix of services outside membership scope, such as laundry management, shopping, repairs, food preparation, childcare, pest treatment or post-construction work.\n- Access, safety, privacy or household rules require a special staffing or visit arrangement.\n\n### The tailored quote process\n\n1. Thank the client and say their home needs a plan designed for them, not a higher surprise bill.\n2. Ask for: neighbourhood, bedrooms, rooms to be covered, visit frequency, whether a first reset is needed, photos/video only if they are comfortable, and preferred days.\n3. Arrange a short walkthrough or video review.\n4. Send one written monthly figure within 24 hours of a completed review. It must state the visits per month, agreed rooms, included tasks, first-reset price if needed, start date and exclusions.\n5. The service lead approves the quote only if the expected delivery fits the team and route.\n6. Review the plan after the first month. Any scope change becomes a new written monthly price from the next billing period.\n\n### What the client should hear\n\n> “We will not charge you by the hour. We first understand the size and routine of your home, then give you one clear monthly price that covers the agreed visits and tasks.”\n\n### Red flags: pause before accepting\n\n- Hazardous waste, pests, serious mould, bodily fluids, chemicals or post-construction debris.\n- A client requests work that could put staff at risk or asks staff to enter without a safe access arrangement.\n- No agreed decision-maker, unclear payment responsibility or a request to discard valuables/personal items.\n\nEscalate these to the service lead. Decline work that cannot be done safely and respectfully.\n\n## WhatsApp sales flow\n\n### 1. New enquiry\n\n> Hello, thank you for contacting **Augusta Home**. We provide regular home cleaning, home organisation and wardrobe support through simple monthly plans.\n> \n> To recommend the right plan, please share: 1) your area, 2) number of bedrooms, 3) whether you prefer two visits or weekly visits, and 4) whether the home needs a first reset.\n\n### 2. Small Home Care offer\n\n> For a compact home, our **Small Home Care** plan is **UGX 200,000 per month**. It includes two scheduled visits, regular cleaning of agreed rooms, kitchen and bathroom care, light tidying and a small organisation touch-up.\n> \n> If that suits you, please share your preferred visit days and area so we can confirm availability.\n\n### 3. Home Care offer\n\n> For your home, I recommend **Home Care at UGX 300,000 per month**. It includes two scheduled visits, regular cleaning of agreed rooms, kitchen, bathroom and living-area organisation, light wardrobe folding/sorting, and a simple checklist after each visit.\n> \n> There is one clear monthly price, not an hourly charge. Would you like us to confirm a start date?\n\n### 4. Weekly Home Care offer\n\n> For a more regular routine, our **Weekly Home Care** plan is **UGX 450,000 per month**. It includes four scheduled visits, regular cleaning of agreed rooms, a rotating home-organisation focus, wardrobe maintenance and a monthly check-in.\n> \n> If you share your area and preferred days, I can check the nearest available slot.\n\n### 5. Tailored Home Care response\n\n> Thank you. Your home would be best served by a **Tailored Home Care** plan, so we can give you a fair fixed monthly price that matches the rooms and routine you need.\n> \n> Please share your area, number of bedrooms, which rooms you want covered, how often you need visits, and whether you would like a first reset. We will review it and send one clear monthly quote.\n\n### 6. First Reset offer\n\n> Before the regular visits begin, we may recommend a **First Home Reset from UGX 150,000**. This gives the home a clean, organised starting point so the monthly plan can stay consistent. We will confirm the exact price before any work begins.\n\n### 7. Booking confirmation\n\n> Your Augusta Home plan is confirmed.\n> \n> **Plan:** [plan name]\n> **Monthly price:** UGX [price]\n> **Visits:** [number] per month\n> **First visit:** [date/time]\n> **Agreed rooms and focus:** [scope]\n> \n> We will send a short checklist after each visit. If anything changes, reply here and we will assist.\n\n### 8. Payment request\n\n> Hello [name], your Augusta Home plan for [month] is **UGX [amount]**. Please make payment using the approved Augusta payment details. Once payment is confirmed, we will keep your scheduled visits active. Thank you.\n\n### 9. Follow-up after no reply\n\n> Hello [name], just checking whether you would still like Augusta Home support for your home. We have monthly plans from **UGX 200,000**, with clear prices and no hourly surprises. If you share your area and home size, I can recommend the right plan.\n\n### 10. After-visit message\n\n> Hello [name], today’s Augusta Home visit is complete. We covered: [short checklist]. For the next visit, we will focus on: [next focus]. Please reply here if you would like any adjustment.\n\n## Sales rules\n\n- Never promise a team, date or price until availability and scope are confirmed.\n- Never negotiate down by silently adding more rooms or work. Offer a smaller plan, fewer rooms, or a tailored plan instead.\n- Never ask a client to send keys, payment PINs, mobile-money codes or sensitive documents by WhatsApp.\n- Record every lead, quote, payment status, booked visit, completed visit, reschedule and complaint in the approved Augusta business system.\n- Keep WhatsApp messages short, clear and friendly. Use the client’s name and repeat the fixed monthly price before booking.\n
