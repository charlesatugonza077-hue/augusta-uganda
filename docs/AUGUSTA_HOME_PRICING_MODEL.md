# Augusta Home pricing model\n\n**Status:** internal pilot price card. Use these figures for quoting and testing, not as a public promise until 3–5 paid pilot memberships confirm visit time, travel and supply usage.\n\n## Pricing position\n\nAugusta Home is a dependable, recurring household-support membership: regular cleaning, practical room organisation and wardrobe care. It is priced per **labour-hour and visit**, not simply by bedroom count. This protects service quality and makes special needs visible before a quote is accepted.\n\nThe figures below are designed for Kampala core service areas. Homes outside the agreed service zone, unusually large homes, heavy soil, pets, construction dust and specialist requests are surveyed and quoted separately.\n\n## Public market check\n\nA public check on 2 November 2026 found broad, inconsistent Kampala references: Safe Work Uganda advertises a 4-hour one-professional essential clean from UGX 60,000; Loop Cleaning lists a basic 2–3-hour visit at UGX 150,000; ProGigFinder lists broad cleaning ranges of UGX 10,000–50,000 per hour depending on home size. These are comparison signals, not interchangeable service specifications.\n\n- [Safe Work Uganda](https://safehandsuganda.com/)\n- [Loop Cleaning Services](https://www.loopcleaningug.com/)\n- [ProGigFinder Kampala guide](https://www.progigfinder.com/cleaning-services-kampala)\n\nAugusta therefore launches in the quality-managed middle: clear scopes, recurring scheduling, known teams where possible, supplies, checklists, privacy standards and home organisation.\n\n## Quote engine\n\n### Base assumptions\n\n| Input | Pilot assumption | Why it matters |\n|---|---:|---|\n| Standard selling rate | UGX 40,000 per labour-hour | Covers skilled, supervised home-support work |\n| Visit delivery allowance | UGX 20,000 per visit | Core-area travel and routine consumables |\n| Monthly coordination allowance | UGX 30,000 | Scheduling, client communication and quality review |\n| Fortnightly membership discount | 5% | Applied after labour and visit allowances |\n| Weekly membership discount | 10% | Rewards predictable recurring work |\n| Direct team cost assumption | UGX 15,000 per labour-hour | Pay, statutory/benefit allowance and cover provision |\n| Transport cost assumption | UGX 12,000 per visit | Used to test route profitability |\n| Consumables cost assumption | UGX 8,000 per visit | Cloths, basic products and replenishment |\n| Admin + quality reserve | 8% of revenue | Coordination, checks and service recovery |\n| Payment reserve | 2% of revenue | Collection and payment costs |\n\n### Formula\n\n`Monthly price = ((monthly labour-hours × 40,000) + (visits × 20,000) + 30,000) × (1 − membership discount)`\n\nRound the final quote **up to the next UGX 5,000**. Keep the result above the minimum monthly membership of **UGX 295,000**.\n\n**Labour-hours = number of team members × hours on site.** For example, two team members working two hours equals four labour-hours.\n\n### Margin test\n\nBefore accepting a membership, test:\n\n`Contribution = price − (labour-hours × 15,000) − (visits × 12,000) − (visits × 8,000) − (price × 10%)`\n\nThe quote should normally retain **at least 40% contribution** before fixed business costs. If it does not, reduce scope, improve routing, raise the price or decline the work.\n\n## Pilot membership menu\n\n| Membership | Best for | Visits | Labour | Included work | Monthly pilot price | Estimated contribution |\n|---|---|---:|---:|---|---:|---:|\n| **Tidy Rhythm** | Compact home or apartment | 2 per month | 3 hours per visit / 6 monthly | Regular clean; kitchen, bathroom and living-space organisation; light wardrobe maintenance; visit checklist | **UGX 295,000** | ~46% |\n| **Home Flow** | Established 1–3 bedroom home | 4 per month | 4 hours per visit / 16 monthly | Regular clean; rotating organisation zones; wardrobe folding and maintenance; checklist and monthly check-in | **UGX 675,000** | ~43% |\n| **Whole Home Reset** | Larger, busy or shared household | 4 per month | 6 hours per visit / 24 monthly | Deeper recurring clean; multiple organisation zones; one full wardrobe reset each month; checklist and monthly review | **UGX 965,000** | ~44% |\n| **Tailored Home** | Large, multi-level or special-scope home | Agreed | Surveyed | Approved work plan only | **Quoted after walkthrough** | ≥40% required |\n\n### Membership scope notes\n\n- The membership price includes ordinary cleaning materials and core-area transport.\n- Clients supply specialist products if they require a particular brand, fragrance-free product or treatment.\n- Wardrobe work means sorting, folding, placing and agreeing keep/donate/decision piles. Augusta never discards clothing or personal items without explicit approval.\n- Each membership includes one primary contact and an agreed visit checklist.\n- Start with the same team where scheduling permits.\n\n## One-time add-ons\n\n| Add-on | Included scope | Pilot price | Pricing rule |\n|---|---|---:|---|\n| **First Reset** | First detailed reset before regular membership, up to 6 labour-hours | **UGX 280,000** | Required where the home needs a baseline clean/organisation |\n| **Wardrobe Reset** | Sort, fold and organise one standard wardrobe zone, up to 4 labour-hours | **UGX 190,000** | Extra zones are surveyed |\n| **Deep-Clean Boost** | Detailed clean of agreed rooms, up to 8 labour-hours | **UGX 360,000** | Excludes hazardous/post-construction work |\n| **Extra home-support hour** | Approved extra cleaning or organisation time | **UGX 45,000** | Sold in 1-hour labour increments |\n| **Outside core-area travel** | Route-specific additional travel | **Quoted** | Added transparently before confirmation |\n\n## Quote workflow\n\n1. Capture home size, location, rooms, access rules, people/pets, priorities and preferred schedule.\n2. Complete a walkthrough or approved video review.\n3. Estimate labour-hours, visit frequency, first-reset need and any add-ons.\n4. Apply the quote engine and contribution test.\n5. Send a written scope: rooms, tasks, visit frequency, team hours, price, start date and exclusions.\n6. Invoice the monthly membership in advance. Charge agreed add-ons before or with the affected visit.\n7. Review actual labour, travel, supplies and client satisfaction after each pilot month.\n\n## Boundaries and change rules\n\n- No fixed public price is guaranteed before the home walkthrough.\n- Deep cleaning, laundry, dry-cleaning coordination, repairs, food preparation, childcare, purchasing, moving, pest treatment, biohazards and post-construction work are outside the standard membership unless specifically quoted.\n- Changes to rooms, frequency, home condition or access may change the next monthly quote.\n- Give at least 24 hours’ notice to reschedule where possible. Repeated late cancellations or failed access are handled under the signed service agreement.\n\n## Pilot review gates\n\nAfter the first **3–5 paid memberships**, compare actual results against the assumptions. Adjust the price card if any of these occur:\n\n- average labour use exceeds quoted labour by more than 15%;\n- route cost is above UGX 12,000 per visit;\n- consumables exceed UGX 8,000 per visit;\n- contribution falls below 40%;\n- recurring client feedback identifies a scope mismatch.\n\nKeep a versioned price card and record the effective date for every pricing change.\n
