# Augusta Home monthly price card\n\n**Status:** internal pilot price card. These are simple monthly starting prices for Kampala core service areas. Give the client one clear price based on their home category and visit schedule. Do not quote labour-hours to clients.\n\n## Pricing principle\n\nAugusta Home sells a fixed monthly household-support plan, not time. Each plan has a clear home size, visit frequency and task list. This lets a household decide quickly: UGX 200,000, UGX 300,000 or UGX 450,000 per month.\n\nIf a home is unusually large, heavily soiled, outside the core area or needs specialised work, the client receives a tailored monthly price after a walkthrough. Never turn a normal visit into an unexpected hourly bill.\n\n## Simple monthly memberships\n\n| Plan | Best fit | Visits each month | What the client gets | Monthly price |\n|---|---|---:|---|---:|\n| **Small Home Care** | Studio, single room or compact 1-bedroom home | 2 visits | Regular clean of agreed rooms, kitchen and bathroom; beds made; surfaces, floors and light tidying; one small organisation touch-up | **UGX 200,000** |\n| **Home Care** | Typical 1–3 bedroom home | 2 visits | Regular cleaning of agreed rooms; kitchen, bathroom and living-area organisation; light wardrobe folding/sorting; completed-visit checklist | **UGX 300,000** |\n| **Weekly Home Care** | Busy household that wants a predictable weekly routine | 4 visits | Weekly regular cleaning of agreed rooms; rotating home-organisation focus; wardrobe maintenance; completed-visit checklist and monthly check-in | **UGX 450,000** |\n| **Tailored Home Care** | Large homes, shared homes or a special routine | Agreed | A written plan built after walkthrough | **Quoted directly** |\n\n## What every standard plan includes\n\n- An agreed recurring cleaning routine.\n- Kitchen, bathroom and living-space tidying within the chosen plan.\n- A simple checklist after every visit.\n- One Augusta contact for rescheduling or special requests.\n- Ordinary cleaning materials and core-area travel.\n\n## Clear limits that protect the price\n\nThe price covers a normal, maintained home and the rooms agreed at onboarding. It does not automatically include a heavy first clean, large-scale decluttering, laundry, dry-cleaning, repairs, shopping, food preparation, childcare, pest treatment, hazardous waste, post-construction cleaning or work outside the agreed rooms.\n\nWardrobe support means folding, sorting and placing clothes. Augusta does not throw away clothing, documents, medication or personal items without the client’s approval.\n\n## Optional fixed-price extras\n\n| Extra | What it covers | Client price |\n|---|---|---:|\n| **First Home Reset** | A detailed first clean and setup before regular visits | **From UGX 150,000** |\n| **Wardrobe Reset** | Full sorting, folding and organisation of one agreed wardrobe area | **From UGX 100,000** |\n| **Extra visit** | One additional standard visit under the existing plan | **From UGX 75,000** |\n| **Large home / outside core area** | Additional scope or route cost | **Quoted before confirmation** |\n\n## How to quote in conversation\n\n1. Ask only: location, bedrooms, how many times per month, and whether the home needs a first reset.\n2. Offer the closest clear plan immediately. Example: "For your two-bedroom home, our Home Care plan is UGX 300,000 per month for two scheduled visits. It includes regular cleaning plus light home and wardrobe organisation."\n3. If the home is a normal fit, confirm the fixed monthly price in writing.\n4. If the home falls outside the plan, do a walkthrough and reply with one tailored monthly figure, not an hourly rate.\n\n## Internal cost guardrails\n\nDo not show these figures to clients. They exist to ensure the friendly fixed price remains sustainable.\n\n| Plan | Working staffing limit | Estimated direct delivery cost | Minimum target margin |\n|---|---|---:|---:|\n| Small Home Care | 2 standard visits; compact home | Up to UGX 125,000 | 37.5% |\n| Home Care | 2 standard visits; normal 1–3 bedroom home | Up to UGX 180,000 | 40% |\n| Weekly Home Care | 4 standard visits; agreed rooms only | Up to UGX 270,000 | 40% |\n\nBefore accepting a plan, the service lead checks that the proposed home fits the listed staffing limit. If it does not, sell the next plan or offer a tailored monthly quote.\n\n## Pilot review\n\nUse these prices for the first 3–5 paid memberships. After one month, review actual travel, supplies, team time, missed visits, complaints and renewal interest. Keep the package structure if clients understand it and each plan reaches its target margin. Change only the plan scope or fixed price, never to surprise hourly charging.\n\n## Market check\n\nPublic Kampala listings reviewed on 2 November 2026 show a wide range: Safe Work Uganda advertises a 4-hour essential clean from UGX 60,000, Loop Cleaning lists a basic 2–3 hour clean at UGX 150,000, and ProGigFinder lists broad cleaning ranges by home size. These are reference points only.
