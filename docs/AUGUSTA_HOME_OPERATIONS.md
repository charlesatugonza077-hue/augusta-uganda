"# Augusta Home: operating model

## Purpose
Augusta Home is a recurring household-support membership. It combines regular cleaning visits, practical home organisation and wardrobe organisation in a consistent, respectful routine. It sits within Augusta as a service line, not as a separate company.

## The client promise
- A clear, agreed visit rhythm.
- A home-specific checklist that guides every visit.
- Cleaning and organising work completed with consent, care and privacy.
- One Augusta contact for questions, changes and service recovery.

## Included membership work
1. Regular scheduled cleaning visits.
2. Kitchen, bathroom and living-space organisation.
3. Wardrobe sorting, folding and clothing organisation.
4. A simple completed-visit checklist.
5. Rescheduling and special requests through the assigned contact.

Any deep cleaning, moving, laundry collection, dry-cleaning, pest treatment, repairs, purchasing, childcare, food preparation or high-risk work must be separately scoped before acceptance.

## Client journey

### 1. Enquiry and fit check
Capture: location/service area, home type and size, number of rooms, preferred days and times, current priorities, access arrangement, pets, allergies, fragile items, existing cleaners, household rules and emergency contact.

Confirm that Augusta can safely serve the location and that the requested work is within scope. Do not promise a price or start date until the home walkthrough is complete.

### 2. Home walkthrough and membership plan
The Augusta lead visits or holds a video walkthrough, with client consent. Agree:
- rooms included and excluded;
- visit frequency and expected visit length;
- cleaning materials supplied by the client versus Augusta;
- organisational priorities and wardrobe boundaries;
- access, security and privacy rules;
- communication channel and the person authorised to change instructions.

Create a one-page Home Profile and a room-by-room recurring checklist. The client approves both before the first visit.

### 3. Quote, agreement and payment
Price from the verified visit plan: team hours, travel, visit frequency, consumables, supervision and any specialist work. Invoice monthly in advance. State cancellation, rescheduling, access-failure and change-of-scope rules in writing.

Do not publish fixed prices until local staffing, travel and supplies costs have been tested.

### 4. First reset visit
The first visit establishes the standard. Photograph nothing unless the client has given written consent. The team lead confirms rooms and priorities on arrival, completes the checklist, flags any limitation immediately and records only operational notes needed for the next visit.

### 5. Recurring visits
- Confirm the upcoming visit the day before.
- Arrive in agreed uniform or identification and use the approved access process.
- Complete the room checklist, then any pre-approved special request.
- Leave the home secure and report completion through the agreed channel.
- Send a short completed-visit checklist, not personal household details.

### 6. Monthly review
The client receives a short check-in: what is working, any recurring issue, changes in rooms or schedule, and whether the membership level still fits.

## Staffing and quality control
- Service lead: client onboarding, walkthroughs, staffing, escalation and quality review.
- Home support team: performs cleaning and organisation tasks to the approved checklist.
- Team lead on each visit: attendance, checklist completion, supply check and incident reporting.

Before assignment: verify identity and references, provide paid practical training, sign confidentiality and conduct expectations, and complete a supervised trial visit. Match the same team to a client whenever possible for consistency.

## Cleaning and organisation standards
- Use a clean-to-dirty, high-to-low workflow and colour-coded cloths where possible.
- Never mix cleaning chemicals. Keep products labelled and out of reach of children and pets.
- Ask before moving documents, medication, cash, jewellery, private devices, intimate items or valuables.
- Never discard an item unless the client has expressly approved it. Use a labelled client-decision basket for uncertain items.
- Do not open closed drawers, cupboards or rooms outside the approved scope.
- Stop and report damage, an unsafe condition, infestation, biohazard, conflict or an access concern.

## Privacy, access and safety
- Keep client Home Profiles, access directions and contact details in the authorised Augusta system only.
- Share keys, codes and access instructions only with the assigned team and only for the current visit.
- Never post client homes, belongings, addresses or stories on social media.
- No unauthorised visitors, food, smoking, alcohol or personal phone use while working.
- Use a check-in and check-out procedure for every team member.
- Log lost property, damage, injury, access failure and client complaint the same day; the service lead contacts the client promptly with the next step.

## Core tools to create before launch
- Household enquiry form fields and fit-check script.
- Home Profile and consent form.
- Room-by-room recurring visit checklist.
- First reset checklist.
- Team attendance and check-in/check-out log.
- Supplies and equipment register.
- Incident, damage and service-recovery log.
- Monthly client review template.

## Pilot launch plan
1. Define service area, team capacity, equipment list and quote calculator.
2. Recruit and train a small pilot team; complete identity and reference checks.
3. Run 3–5 paid pilot memberships with detailed feedback.
4. Measure visit length, travel time, supply use, repeat requests, complaints and renewal intent.
5. Adjust scopes, checklists, pricing and staffing before broader promotion.

## Metrics
Track active memberships, visits completed as scheduled, average visit duration, repeat special requests, reschedules, client satisfaction, complaints resolved within the agreed window, team retention and membership renewal rate.
