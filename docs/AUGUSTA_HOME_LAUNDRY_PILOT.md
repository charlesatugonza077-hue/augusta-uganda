# Augusta Home Laundry Pilot

## Purpose
Pilot a separate, in-home laundry add-on for Augusta Home. Clients can book it alone or add it to a cleaning visit. Augusta comes to the home with its own machine, washes and spin-dries ordinary household laundry, hangs it in the client’s safe drying area, then folds and arranges it during the next scheduled visit.

## Pilot scope
Run for four weeks with 3–5 clients on one manageable Kampala route. Only accept ordinary washable clothing, towels, bedsheets and light blankets. Do not accept dry-clean-only garments, silk, leather, suits, heavily stained clothes, valuables without written care instructions, or items that cannot be safely washed on site. Do not iron during this pilot.

## Pilot services and prices
| Service | What happens | Pilot price |
|---|---|---:|
| Laundry Wash & Hang | One visit: sort, wash, spin-dry and hang one standard load. | UGX 35,000 |
| Laundry Wash, Hang & Fold | Wash and hang on visit one; fold and arrange on the client’s next scheduled Augusta visit. | UGX 55,000 |
| Extra standard load | Second comparable load during the same visit, if time and hanging space allow. | UGX 20,000 |
| Bedding or bulky item | Price agreed before work starts. | Fixed quote |

A standard load is up to the safe working capacity set after test runs. Do not use kilogram pricing in the pilot. Confirm one fixed price by WhatsApp before booking. The UGX 55,000 service assumes the fold is paired with a scheduled Augusta Home visit or another nearby confirmed stop. For a stand-alone return visit, give a tailored fixed price before confirming.

## What the client provides
Reliable power, enough water, usable drainage, a safe drying area, access at the agreed time and clear instructions for sensitive items. The client must remove valuables, cash, jewellery, documents, electronics and sharp objects from pockets and laundry.

## What Augusta provides
The machine, detergent, gloves, laundry bags and normal wash handling. Augusta may pause or reschedule for unsafe access, missing water/power, unusable drainage or drying space, or a machine fault.

## Delivery flow
1. Capture location, preferred time, number/type of loads, and confirm water, power, drainage and hanging space.
2. Confirm the fixed price and whether folding is paired with the next Augusta visit.
3. At arrival, sort by colour and care needs; record a simple bag/item count. Take a before photo only with client permission.
4. Wash and spin-dry separated loads. Spin-dry extracts water; it does not fully dry clothes.
5. Hang in the agreed drying area. Never leave wet clothes in bags or the machine.
6. Send a short day-one completion message with the planned fold time.
7. At the next visit, confirm clothes are dry, then fold and arrange them. Escalate damp items instead of folding them.
8. Mark complete only after handover; record payment reference, income, detergent/transport costs, incidents and feedback in BridgePoint Pulse.

## Equipment decision
Buy a 7 kg twin-tub semi-automatic unit from an established Kampala seller, ideally the SPJ if the listed two-year manufacturer warranty is confirmed on the receipt. Twin-tub machines require staff to move washed clothes into the spin tub, but they are more practical for mobile client work than tiny foldable machines. Before payment, inspect the unit, have the seller demonstrate wash, drain and spin, confirm 220–240 V operation, take the warranty terms and receipt, and check size/weight against transport arrangements.

## Pilot budget and review
Initial budget: machine up to UGX 600,000 plus detergent, gloves, laundry bags, pegs, extension cable and a simple first-aid/cleaning kit. At UGX 55,000 per Wash, Hang & Fold service, 11 bookings generate UGX 605,000 revenue before transport, detergent and labour. That is a revenue milestone, not profit.

After four weeks review paid bookings, repeat clients, time per load and fold, transport/consumables per job, complaints or damage, machine issues, profitability of stand-alone fold visits and demand for adding laundry to regular Home Care. Continue only if all costs leave a positive margin.

## Client-facing positioning
“Augusta Home can do more than clean. You can also book a separate in-home laundry visit: we wash, spin-dry and hang your clothes at home, then fold and arrange them on your next scheduled visit.”

Always say “wash, spin-dry and hang”, not “full dryer service.”
