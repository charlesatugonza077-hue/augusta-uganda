# Augusta Home booking payment: Uganda legal review

**Status: internal working note, not legal advice.** Prepared 3 November 2026 for counsel review before Augusta activates any payment or cancellation term. The public site correctly treats its policy pages as proposed.

## Bottom line

Use **"UGX 30,000 booking payment"** or **"commitment payment"**, not "non-refundable fee". It is credited in full to the first confirmed Augusta Home service payment. Do not describe it as automatically non-refundable or deduct it for a customer cancellation until Ugandan counsel confirms the enforceability of the exact contract and sales flow. A different label does not remove statutory refund rights.

Until legal review and operational controls are complete, do not collect this payment through the website. A customer may still enquire and receive a written quote and booking summary.

## Why this is the cautious position

1. **Electronic Transactions Act, Cap. 99, ss. 23–25 and 27.** A web, WhatsApp or other computer-mediated booking may be an electronic transaction. Section 23 requires suppliers to display, among other things, identity and legal status, physical address and telephone, email or website, sufficient service description, full price and extra charges, payment method, terms, timing, record access, refund policy, payment/data security and privacy policy. The customer must be able to review, correct and withdraw before placing an order. Failure to make the section 23 disclosures or provide those steps can create a 14-day cancellation right and a refund obligation.

2. **Seven-day cancellation and refund.** Section 24 gives a consumer a seven-day cancellation right for an electronic transaction for goods or services, measured from receiving the service or concluding the agreement, and says prepaid money is refundable within 30 days. The Act includes exceptions in section 23(7), but their interaction with the section 24 cancellation right and a household-service booking needs counsel confirmation. Augusta should not rely on a generic "non-refundable" term or a pre-ticked consent to defeat that right.

3. **Performance and availability.** Under section 27, unless another period is agreed, an electronic order is to be executed within 30 days. If Augusta cannot supply the service, it must notify the customer before the agreed time and refund payments within 30 days.

4. **Supply of services.** The Sale of Goods and Supply of Services Act, 2018 (Cap. 292), sections 3, 5, 9, 11, 18 and 46 recognise service contracts in data-message form, permit an agreed price, require reasonable care and skill, and imply performance within a reasonable time where no time is fixed. Express terms do not replace the Act’s implied terms.

5. **Personal data.** Booking contact details, address and payment references need a clear privacy notice before collection and secure handling. Obtain specific legal advice on the Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019 and 2021 Regulations, including registration, retention and processor arrangements. Do not store mobile-money PINs or card credentials.

## Proposed customer wording for counsel to approve

> **UGX 30,000 booking payment**
> For every Augusta Home package, a UGX 30,000 booking payment is required before we confirm a visit. It is credited in full toward your first service payment.
>
> **Before you pay**
> We will give you the service scope, visit date or timing, total price and any approved extra charge in writing. Please check the booking details and ask us to correct anything before you confirm.
>
> **Changes and cancellation**
> Tell us as soon as possible if you need to change or cancel. Nothing in this policy limits a right to cancel or receive a refund that Ugandan law gives you. Until our terms have been reviewed and approved, Augusta will not treat the booking payment as automatically non-refundable.
>
> **If Augusta cannot deliver**
> If we cannot provide a confirmed service, we will tell you promptly and offer a suitable reschedule, replacement or refund, as appropriate and required by law.
>
> **Refunds**
> Any refund due under the law or our confirmed terms will be paid using a secure method and within the applicable legal period.

## Minimum go-live checklist

- Confirm Augusta’s registered legal name and status, physical service address, phone number and working email.
- Publish full package prices, UGX 30,000 payment, what it is credited against, payment method, all extra charges, service timing and minimum duration for recurring plans.
- Put the exact approved terms, cancellation/refund route, privacy notice and complaint contact beside the payment step, in a form the client can save.
- Ensure the booking flow lets the client review the booking, correct errors and withdraw before placing it.
- Send a dated booking record and payment receipt, retaining evidence of the selected service, price, cancellation request and refund.
- Set one operational rule: if Augusta cannot supply a confirmed service, stop escalation, notify the client promptly and route the payment for refund review.
- Obtain written Ugandan legal advice before any retained-payment rule, no-show charge, short-notice cancellation rule or waiver/consent wording goes live.
