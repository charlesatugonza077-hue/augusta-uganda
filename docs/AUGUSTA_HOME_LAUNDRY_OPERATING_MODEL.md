# Augusta Home Laundry: Transport and Operating Model

## Core decision
Laundry is a separately bookable Augusta Home add-on. It is not included in ordinary cleaning prices. It can be added to a cleaning visit, but it has its own all-inclusive price.

The client sees one fixed price. Augusta builds local transport into that price; it does not add a separate boda charge after the client has chosen the service.

## Service zones and route discipline
Start with two routes only:

- **Kira-side route:** Kyaliwajjala, Najjera, Kira and nearby connected residential areas.
- **Kampala-side route:** Makerere, Kawempe and nearby connected areas.

Use one zone per working day. Do not cross between the two zones during one shift. Confirm every home from a location pin before accepting it. Homes outside the active route receive a single tailored all-inclusive quote or are booked for a later route day.

The machine must not be taken to one isolated home at a loss. Group 3–4 laundry homes in the same general area on each service day. If carrying the machine requires a higher-cost boda, tuk-tuk or car arrangement, price that cost into the single client quote before accepting the booking.

## Ten-home operating model

This is the first scale point: ten households taking Weekly Laundry Care at UGX 240,000 a month. Each household receives four cycles, creating 40 laundry cycles monthly. At four homes per route day, that means about ten route days each month, or 2–3 route days per week.

| Monthly item | Assumption | Working cost |
|---|---|---:|
| Revenue | 10 homes × UGX 240,000 | UGX 2,400,000 |
| Laundry worker | Part-time recurring worker | UGX 600,000–700,000 |
| Transport | 10 route days; allow UGX 15,000/day, not merely depot return travel | UGX 150,000 |
| Detergent and wash consumables | 40 loads × UGX 5,000 starting allowance | UGX 200,000 |
| Machine reserve | Wear, servicing and small repairs | UGX 100,000 |
| Admin/payment buffer | Airtime, payment charges and small losses | UGX 50,000 |
| Estimated direct operating cost | All items above | UGX 1,100,000–1,200,000 |
| Contribution before owner pay, tax and major repairs | Revenue less direct operating cost | UGX 1,200,000–1,300,000 |

The direct operating cost is approximately UGX 110,000–120,000 per recurring client per month. At UGX 240,000 per client, this leaves roughly UGX 120,000–130,000 per client for owner pay, business profit, tax and larger unexpected costs. This is a planning model, not a guarantee: replace every assumption with actual pilot records.

## Labour plan

Ten recurring weekly homes may support one part-time laundry worker at UGX 600,000–700,000 monthly **only** when routes are clustered and folding happens alongside planned Home Care visits. This is not a reason to hire a full-time worker immediately.

For the first four-week pilot, pay per route day or per completed job, record the real hours and then set a monthly arrangement only after ten recurring weekly clients have paid for two consecutive months. At ten homes, UGX 600,000 labour equals UGX 60,000 per client per month; UGX 700,000 equals UGX 70,000.

## Consumables

The client provides water and electricity because laundry is done at their home. Augusta provides detergent, basic stain/white-item support where agreed, gloves, laundry bags and replacement pegs.

## Operational safeguards

- A staff member confirms water, power, drainage and safe hanging space before leaving for the home.
- “Spin-dry” means water extraction, not full heat drying. Clothes are hung at the client’s home.
- Do not fold damp clothes.
- Exclude dry-clean-only garments, leather, silk, suits, valuables and sharp objects from ordinary service.
- Record actual transport, detergent, time and client feedback for every job in BridgePoint Pulse.
- Review the price after the first 20 completed loads, not based on guesswork.
