# Augusta Home — approved current operating and pricing model

**Status:** Current operating model confirmed by the Director's representative on 3 November 2026. This document supersedes conflicting historical drafts for current quoting and delivery.

## 1. Customer promise

Augusta Home is a monthly household-support plan. The monthly fee buys four scheduled check-ins across the month: one check-in each week.

The monthly fee includes the regular home-support work agreed during onboarding and recorded in the household checklist. It is not a fee merely for arriving at the property.

## 2. Included monthly service

The exact rooms and recurring tasks are agreed with the customer before the first visit. The regular checklist can include:
- Routine cleaning of the agreed rooms and surfaces.
- Floor care, sweeping and mopping.
- Regular bathroom care and cleaning.
- Kitchen and living-area tidying within the agreed scope.
- Light home organisation and routine wardrobe maintenance.
- A short completion checklist after each weekly check-in.

The monthly plan is delivered through four weekly check-ins. The same agreed checklist and household boundaries guide each visit. Do not promise work outside the approved checklist.

## 3. Separately priced service

Laundry and ironing are not included in the standard monthly fee. Their fees are agreed with the customer before booking, taking account of:
- Number and type of items or loads.
- Washing, drying/hanging, folding and ironing required.
- Whether the work is added to an existing Home visit or needs a separate trip.
- Location, equipment, time and other delivery costs.

Do not publish or promise a universal laundry/ironing price unless a current approved price card is issued.

## 4. Optional combined package

Customers may request an all-inclusive monthly package combining:
- Four weekly Home check-ins.
- The agreed regular cleaning and organisation checklist.
- Laundry and ironing within a clearly stated volume and service limit.

This package must be quoted individually until management approves a standard price and included laundry allowance. The written quote must specify item/load limits, ironing scope, collection/return or in-home method, any excess-load charge, and what happens when volume exceeds the allowance.

## 5. Published monthly Home plan prices

The current public price points remain unchanged:
- UGX 200,000/month — studio or one-bedroom apartment.
- UGX 300,000/month — two-bedroom apartment.
- UGX 450,000/month — three-bedroom apartment.

Each price covers four weekly check-ins and the agreed regular home-support checklist for the relevant home category. Standalone residential houses and homes outside the standard scope receive a tailored monthly quote after assessment.

## 6. Separately quoted work

Quote separately and obtain customer approval before accepting:
- Laundry and ironing.
- Extra check-ins beyond the four monthly visits.
- Heavy first-time reset or deep-cleaning work beyond normal recurring upkeep.
- Large-scale decluttering, specialist cleaning, repairs, hazardous work or tasks outside the agreed checklist.
- A materially larger home or additional rooms not included in the agreed plan.

Routine cleaning, mopping, bathroom care, light tidying and ordinary home/wardrobe organisation within the agreed checklist must not be represented as separate add-ons.

## 7. Booking and delivery procedure

1. Record the customer's location, home type, number of bedrooms, rooms to cover and preferred weekly visit day.
2. Walk through the home or conduct a consented video assessment where needed.
3. Agree the recurring checklist, supplies responsibility, access rules, household boundaries and any exclusions.
4. Confirm the monthly fee and four weekly check-in dates in writing.
5. Record laundry/ironing separately if requested, with the agreed fee and scope.
6. If the customer chooses the combined package, state its full price and laundry allowance in writing.
7. Collect payment according to the approved booking/payment policy.
8. Confirm each visit, complete the checklist, record exceptions and send the customer a short completion update.
9. Review the arrangement monthly and document any agreed scope or price change before the next billing period.

## 8. Customer-facing explanation

“Augusta Home is a monthly plan. You pay one agreed monthly fee and we check in at your home once every week — four visits across the month. The fee covers the regular cleaning and home-organisation tasks we agree for your home. Laundry and ironing are priced separately, or we can prepare a combined monthly package that includes them. We confirm the exact scope and price before you book.”

## 9. Pricing governance

The published UGX 200,000, UGX 300,000 and UGX 450,000 prices have not been changed by this update. No combined-package price is invented here. Management should approve a laundry allowance, cost assumptions and package price before advertising a fixed all-inclusive amount.
