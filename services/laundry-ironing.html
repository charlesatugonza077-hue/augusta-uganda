Augusta Home

Laundry care planned around your household routine.

We come with a machine for ordinary washable laundry, then wash, spin-dry and hang it safely in your home.

Enquire about this serviceShare home details

What is included

Support with a clear scope before the work starts.

We check availability and confirm the rooms, loads or wardrobe areas before booking.

  • Mobile laundry: UGX 45,000 per standard load, whether booked with an Augusta Home visit or as a stand-alone service
  • The fee covers washing, spin-drying and hanging ordinary washable household items
  • Folding, unusually large loads, access constraints or other special requirements are agreed separately before booking
How does the mobile laundry service work?

We bring the machine, wash and spin-dry ordinary household laundry, then hang it in your safe drying area. Laundry is separate from the weekly visit plans. Folding can be booked for the next scheduled Augusta Home visit.

What does the home need to provide?

Safe access to water, power, drainage and a usable drying area. We confirm these before booking.

What items are not accepted?

Dry-clean-only garments, silk, leather, suits, valuables without care instructions, and heavily stained items are not accepted in the pilot.
