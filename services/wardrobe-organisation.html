What is included

Support with a clear scope before the work starts.

We check availability and confirm the rooms, loads or wardrobe areas before booking.

  • Full sorting and folding of one agreed wardrobe area
  • Simple categories that make items easier to find
  • Personal items are never discarded without approval
  • Light wardrobe maintenance is included in selected Home Care plans
Will Augusta throw away items?

No. We do not discard clothing, documents, medication or personal items without your approval.

Is it part of a cleaning plan?

Light folding and sorting is included in Home Care. A full reset is priced separately from UGX 100,000.

Can we organise more than one area?

Yes. We will agree the areas and provide one clear quote before starting.
