Augusta Home

Weekly apartment visits with cleaning support included.

Choose a weekly apartment plan with agreed cleaning, mopping, bathroom care, utensil washing and light wardrobe support included in each visit. Mobile laundry is a separately paid add-on.

Enquire about this serviceShare home details

What is included

Support with a clear scope before the work starts.

We check availability and confirm the rooms, loads or wardrobe areas before booking.

  • Small Weekly Home Visit Plan: UGX 200,000/month for a studio apartment or one-bedroom apartment with one living room and one bedroom; one scheduled visit each week for four weeks
  • Weekly Home Visit Plan: UGX 300,000/month for a two-bedroom apartment with two bedrooms and one living room; one scheduled visit each week for four weeks
  • Complete Weekly Home Visit Plan: UGX 450,000/month for a three-bedroom apartment; one scheduled visit each week for four weeks
  • Standalone residential houses receive a site visit and tailored quote. The weekly apartment plans include agreed house cleaning, mopping, bathroom cleaning and scrubbing, utensil washing and light wardrobe support. Mobile laundry is a separately paid add-on
What do I share to get the right plan?

Your area, whether it is an apartment or standalone residential house, the number of bedrooms and living rooms, your weekly visit schedule, and whether the home needs a first reset.

How are standalone residential houses priced?

We visit the property first, assess the size and scope, then provide a tailored quote before any booking is confirmed.

Do you charge by the hour?

The weekly apartment plan has one fixed monthly price for four scheduled visits. Each visit includes agreed cleaning, mopping, bathroom care, utensil washing and light wardrobe support. Extra work outside the agreed scope is discussed and quoted before confirmation.

What is outside the regular plan?

Extra visits, heavy first cleaning, large-scale decluttering, repairs, hazardous work and specialist services are discussed and quoted before confirmation. Mobile laundry remains a separate paid add-on. Standalone residential houses require a site visit and tailored quote.
