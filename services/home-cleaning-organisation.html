Augusta Home

A cleaner, calmer home on a routine that fits.

Regular cleaning of agreed rooms, plus practical order where it helps daily life most.

Enquire about this serviceShare home details

What is included

Support with a clear scope before the work starts.

We check availability and confirm the rooms, loads or wardrobe areas before booking.

  • Small Home Care: UGX 200,000/month for two visits
  • Home Care: UGX 300,000/month for two visits
  • Weekly Home Care: UGX 450,000/month for four visits
  • First Home Reset from UGX 150,000 where needed
What do I share to get the right plan?

Your area, number of bedrooms, preferred visit rhythm and whether the home needs a first reset.

Do you charge by the hour?

No. We confirm one fixed monthly price for the agreed rooms, visits and tasks.

What is outside the regular plan?

Heavy first cleaning, large-scale decluttering, repairs, hazardous work and specialist services are discussed and quoted before confirmation.

Starting price

From UGX 200,000 per month

Share a few home details and we will confirm whether the standard plan fits or a tailored quote is better.
